  • The fine is Rs. 2 for each day after the due date.
  • Returning on or before the due date costs nothing.
  • Example: 5 days late is Rs. 10.
{% endcall %} {% call rule('reserve', 'Reservations and holds') %}
  • Only books with no copy on the shelf can be reserved.
  • A member can hold one reservation per book.
  • The queue is first come, first served.
  • When a copy is returned, the first member waiting gets it on hold for 7 days.
  • If they don't collect it in time, it goes to the next member, or back on the shelf.

  • The desk can view the queue but can't cancel reservations.
